✨ About This Project • Institutional Vision

🎓 Alumni Tracking System

The official digital ecosystem engineered for Istanbul University (İstanbul Üniversitesi) to organize, search, publish announcements for, and manage alumni profiles, career opportunities, and candidate applications across generations.

🏛️ Carrying the 1453 Beyazıt Legacy into the Digital Era

Founded in 1453, Istanbul University is Turkey's oldest and most prestigious institution of higher education. This platform bridges graduates across Computer & Software Engineering, Law, Medicine, Economics, Science, and Letters—connecting seasoned industry leaders with emerging alumni talent.

🎯 Purpose & Core Capabilities

The primary goal of this application is to establish a centralized portal for managing alumni data and maintaining institutional connections. It facilitates:

  • Profile Management: Storing graduation year, academic department, contact details, and nested career/application history (/users).
  • Campus Announcements & Traditions: Publishing Beyazıt Homecoming (*Mezunlar Günü*) bulletins, seminars, and academic news (/announcements).
  • Career & Job Exchange: Enabling alumni-for-alumni hiring with strict domain validation rules JP-R1–JP-R7 (/jobs).
  • Applicant Tracking (ATS): Managing candidate CVs, cover notes, and evaluation workflows APP-R1–APP-R6 (/applications).

💻 Tech Stack & Architecture

Built on a decoupled, production-grade Model-View-Controller (MVC) architecture with enterprise fault tolerance:

  • Frontend (HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript): Responsive Refero & Awesome-Design-MD token system, real-time table filtering, and asynchronous API health probes.
  • Backend (Node.js & Express 5): Serves both server-side rendered (SSR) HTML views and RESTful JSON APIs documented with OpenAPI 3.0 (/api/swagger).
  • Database (PostgreSQL 16 & Fallback): Centralized pg.Pool connection pooling with automated SQL migrations, data factories, and resilient in-memory fallback.
  • Guardian Pipeline & Telemetry: 404 route-typo diagnosis, safe /404?from=... redirection, 5xx ERR-* Incident ID tracking, and dual-sink logging to logs/error.logs.
