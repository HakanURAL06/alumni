🛡️ Role Capabilities (RBAC)

  • 🎓 Alumni (Mezun — Default)

    Full directory profile, publish career opportunities (POST /jobs), evaluate candidates applying to your jobs, apply to peer postings, and mentor students.

  • 🎒 Current Student (Mevcut Öğrenci)

    Explore alumni mentors, apply to active internships and entry-level jobs (POST /applications), and manage your application dossier.

  • 💼 Employer Partner (İşveren)

    Publish and manage corporate job openings, review applicant CVs (/jobs/:id/applications), and update candidate evaluation statuses.

📋 Validation Rules (ddd.md)

  • USR-R1 Case-insensitive unique RFC 5322 email address.
  • USR-R2 Graduation year integer within 1900..2100.
  • USR-R3 Canonical role enum assignment (defaults to alumni).
  • USR-R4 Self-escalation to moderator or admin is strictly blocked (403 Forbidden).
  • USR-R7 Accounts initialize in active state upon validation.
